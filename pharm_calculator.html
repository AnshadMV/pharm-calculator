5. IP GST-included handling

Active only when IPPharmacyAmountGSTInclude = 1 and EncounterSubMode = IP.

  • Effective CGST and SGST percentages become 0%.
  • CGST, SGST and total GST amounts become ₹0.00.
  • Base Charge equals MRP because no GST is extracted.
  • Discount adjustment remains negative; taxable and final amounts reflect the discount.
  • Contract Charge = Final Amount − Adjustment Amount.

The document also says IP selling price should remain constant. This page follows the explicit formulas and contract-charge rule provided; confirm the exact production meaning of “Final Amount” for IP billing before financial sign-off.
