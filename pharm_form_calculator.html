4. Formula reference

Standard calculation sequence. Adjustment is stored as a negative amount.

StepFormulaPurpose
1. Base ChargeMRP ÷ (1 + (CGST% + SGST%) ÷ 100)Removes GST included in MRP.
2. Adjustment−Base × Discount% ÷ 100Negative discount amount; zero when no discount.
3. Taxable AmountBase + AdjustmentTaxable price after discount.
4. CGST / SGSTTaxable × respective rate ÷ 100Tax computed after discount.
5. Total GSTCGST + SGSTCombined GST amount.
6. Final AmountTaxable + Total GSTBill amount after discount and tax.
7. Contract ChargeFinal Amount − Adjustment AmountApplies the supplied contract-charge rule.
Example: MRP ₹100 · CGST 6% · SGST 6% · Discount 10% → Base ₹89.29 · Adjustment −₹8.93 · Taxable ₹80.36 · CGST ₹4.82 · SGST ₹4.82 · Total GST ₹9.64 · Final ₹90.00.

5. IP GST-included handling

Active only when IPPharmacyAmountGSTInclude = 1 and EncounterSubMode = IP.

  • Effective CGST and SGST rates are set to 0%.
  • CGST, SGST and total GST amounts are ₹0.00.
  • Base Charge equals MRP because GST is not extracted.
  • Adjustment remains negative; taxable and final amounts reflect the discount.
  • Contract Charge = Final Amount − Adjustment Amount.

The source document also says IP selling price should remain constant. Validate the exact production definition of Final Amount and Contract Charge before financial sign-off.
